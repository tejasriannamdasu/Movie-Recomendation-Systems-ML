Built a hybrid movie recommender (academic project) on MovieLens latest-small (100k ratings, 610 users) combining TF-IDF content filtering over genres and tags with mean-centred item-based collaborative filtering and SVD; cut RMSE by 10.0% versus a user-mean baseline (0.9592 to 0.8629) on a leakage-free time-based split, and showed leakage would have made RMSE look 34.6% better.
